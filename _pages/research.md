## Introduction
My REU summer research project through the Center for Interdisciplinary Exploration and Research in Astrophysics (CIERA) (Northwestern University, 2026) was to analyze and constrain circumstellar dust properties around Red Supergiant (RSG) supernova progenitor candidates in M51 and M82 by determining the fraction of RSG candidates better explained by graphite-dominated versus silicate-dominated circumstellar dust models.
